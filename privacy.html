Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use our mobile application developed by Sonal Udeshi.

Your privacy is important to us. This application is designed to collect minimal data and respect user privacy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Photos or personal files
  • Precise location

Usage Data

The application may collect anonymous usage information to help improve the performance and functionality of the app.

This may include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Feature usage statistics
  • Crash diagnostics
  • Interaction events

This information is not linked to your identity.

Analytics

The application uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with the app.

This service may collect anonymous data such as:

  • Device information
  • App usage patterns
  • Session duration
  • General location based on IP address

This information helps us improve the application and fix issues.

You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Our applications offer a free tier with optional monthly subscriptions for premium features. All payments are processed securely by Apple through the App Store.

We do not collect, store, or process any payment or billing information. Apple handles all transaction data in accordance with their privacy policy.

Data Storage

Most app preferences and settings remain stored locally on your device. Analytics data is processed by Google according to their privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

The application may use the following third-party service:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase

Children's Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
